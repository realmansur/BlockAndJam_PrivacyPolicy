Block and Jam – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Block and Jam (the “Game”), developed by HMD Softworks (“we”, “us”), handles information. In short: we do not collect any personal data.

✔No personal data collected
✔No ads
✔No analytics or tracking
✔No account or sign-in
✔Works fully offline
✔Nothing is shared or sold

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal details, and it does not use device identifiers or advertising IDs.

2. Information stored only on your device

To save your progress, the Game keeps a small amount of game data locally on your device using the device’s standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. It includes:

  • Game progress: levels unlocked, stars, coins, boosters and lives.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration and colour-symbol preferences.
  • Profile choices: your chosen avatar and an optional player name you can type in. The name is only shown inside the Game on your device.
  • Simple statistics shown in your in-game profile (for example, levels cleared).

3. Internet access

The Game does not request internet access and works completely offline. It does not connect to our servers or to any third-party servers.

4. Advertising and analytics

The Game contains no advertisements and uses no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services. There are no in-app purchases with real money; coins are earned only by playing.

5. Third-party services

The Game does not include any third-party services that collect data. The Game is distributed through Google Play; your use of Google Play is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

The Game does not request any sensitive device permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access).

7. Children’s privacy

The Game is suitable for players of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

8. Deleting your data

All game data is stored only on your device. You can delete it at any time by using Settings → Reset Progress in the Game, by clearing the app’s data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the Game.

9. Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed. Game data on your device is protected by your device’s own security.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. We encourage you to review it from time to time.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact HMD Softworks at hmdsoftworks@gmail.com.
